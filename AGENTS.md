# Research session contract

Read `RESEARCH_STATE.json` and its `required_session_reads` before changing research code or interpreting results. Update that state and append a development record before finishing.

Preserve previous results, failed runs, frozen protocols, source identities and decisions. Engineering correctness does not establish a scientific result or reopen a closed predecessor. Respect the specific protected boundary recorded in state. Use constructed fixtures for implementation checks. A new hypothesis needs a separately versioned specification, falsifier, finite development budget and pre-outcome confirmatory freeze.
